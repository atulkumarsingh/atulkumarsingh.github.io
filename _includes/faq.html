Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the process, timeframes, and expected results.

How long does it take to see results?

Most initial optimizations and technical tracking fixes take 1-2 weeks. You'll generally start seeing improved performance and cleaner data within the first 30 days of the strategy going live.

Do you manage the ad spend directly?

Yes. I handle the entire media buying process across Google and Meta, including campaign structure, budget allocation, bidding, and creative testing inside your ad accounts.

Can you integrate my existing CRM with your ads strategy?

Absolutely. While I specialize in Zoho CRM architecture, the underlying logic of sending offline conversion data back to ad platforms applies to HubSpot, Salesforce, or any system with an open API.

What is the required minimum ad spend?

To properly leverage machine learning algorithms across multiple channels, I typically recommend a minimum starting budget that supports mathematical significance in testing depending on your industry's CPA.
